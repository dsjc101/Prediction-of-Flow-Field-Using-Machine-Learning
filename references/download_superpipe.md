# Princeton Superpipe Experimental Data Access

The Stage 5 turbulent pipe evaluations benchmark against experimental Pitot traverse velocity profiles from the Princeton Superpipe experiment.

- **Source:** Princeton Gas Dynamics Laboratory (Prof. A. J. Smits, M. V. Zagarola, B. J. McKeon et al.)
- **Official URL:** [https://smits.princeton.edu/superpipe-pitot-data](https://smits.princeton.edu/superpipe-pitot-data)

### Included Test Profiles
The 8 velocity traverse files corresponding to Reynolds numbers $31{,}577 \le \mathrm{Re} \le 145{,}790$ are:
1. `zag_3.1577E+04.txt`
2. `zag_4.0628E+04.txt`
3. `zag_5.4216E+04.txt`
4. `zag_7.3698E+04.txt`
5. `zag_9.8519E+04.txt`
6. `zag_1.4579E+05.txt`
7. `mck_7.4264E+04.txt`
8. `mck_1.4458E+05.txt`

These data files are publicly hosted by Princeton University for academic and research validation.
